### ISO 50001 Compliance Status – Human-Centric EnMS Implementation at LAUDS Factories

The ISO 50001:2018 standard provides a shared framework through which organizations of every kind can establish, implement, and continually improve energy management. Its opening statement makes this intent unambiguous: the standard “is applicable to any organization regardless of its type, size, complexity, geographical location, or products and services provided.” 
Within this understanding, Human-Centric Energy Management System (Human-Centric EnMS) assists how ISO 50001 can be interpreted in FabLabs, maker spaces, and microfactories – the so-called LAUDS Factories – by transforming management clauses into participatory practices. As the standard itself requires documentation of procedures, reviews, forms and records, LAUDS factories are expected to develop that documentation to use along with the Human-Centric EnMS.
