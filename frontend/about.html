⚡ Human-Centric EnMS

Energy Management & Digital Product Passport System

About This Platform

The Human-Centric Energy Management System was developed under the LAUDS Exploration Open Call in response to the challenge "Unlocking Insights: Revolutionizing the Way We See and Manage Resource Consumption". This project adapts industrial energy management practices into a human-centric, open-source demonstrator suitable for FabLabs, maker spaces, and microfactories.

This platform integrates real-time IoT data collection, advanced analytics, and artistic visualization tools to make energy and resource flows not only measurable but also understandable and meaningful for a wider community. By combining technical interventions with artistic contributions, we transform abstract data into visual, narrative, and spatial experiences.

Deployed and validated across three FabCity Hamburg sites — JUPITER OpenSpace, Fabulous St. Pauli, and FABRIC Workshop — the system was tested in real-world settings and showcased through workshops with diverse audiences.

Our Mission

To make energy and resource flows not only measurable but also understandable and meaningful for FabLabs, maker spaces, and microfactories. We empower communities with intelligent, data-driven solutions that optimize energy efficiency, reduce carbon footprint, and enable transparent product lifecycle management through a human-centric approach that combines technical precision with artistic expression.

By making the invisible flows of energy and materials tangible in physical space, we inspire creativity, community engagement, and sustainable practices that are both functional and relatable.

Technical & Artistic Integration

The Human-Centric EnMS uniquely combines technical excellence with artistic expression to create an engaging and accessible energy management experience:

Technical Components

  • Modular IoT Hardware: ESP32 sensor hubs, smart plugs (Shelly), thermocouples, and humidity sensors capture comprehensive data on energy use and environmental conditions
  • Backend Infrastructure: Node-RED, PostgreSQL/TimescaleDB, and MQTT protocols enable real-time data storage and analysis
  • Dual Dashboard Experience: Grafana dashboards provide both advanced technical views and simplified monitoring for diverse user skill levels
  • Legacy Integration: Raspberry Pi bridges connect older 3D printers through SimplyPrint.io cloud platform

Artistic Contributions (Arti Sanat)

  • Energy Plant Visualizations: Natural motifs (potato, corn, sugar cane, filament spools) symbolize material cycles and energy flows through growth stages
  • LED Panel Installations: 55×55 cm panels with backlighting and explanatory texts make sustainability tangible and accessible
  • Animation & Storytelling: Co-created with Hamburg stakeholders as the main medium for awareness raising
  • Community Workshops: Public engagement through games, prototyping, painting, and digital art linking creativity with sustainability

Key Features

Real-Time Monitoring

Live energy consumption tracking across multiple devices and production lines with sub-second granularity.

Digital Product Passports

Complete product lifecycle documentation including energy usage, materials, and environmental impact.

AI-Powered Analytics

Machine learning algorithms for predictive maintenance and energy optimization recommendations.

Sustainability Metrics

Comprehensive carbon footprint calculation and ESG reporting aligned with international standards.

Enterprise Security

Role-based access control, encrypted data transmission, and audit logging for compliance.

IoT Integration

Seamless connectivity with sensors, PLCs, and industrial equipment via MQTT, Modbus, and OPC UA.

Technology Stack

Core Technologies

  • Grafana: Advanced visualization and dashboarding
  • Node-RED: IoT workflow automation and data integration
  • TimescaleDB: Time-series database for high-performance data storage
  • Python & Flask: Backend API and machine learning services
  • PostgreSQL: Relational database for structured data
  • Mosquitto MQTT: Message broker for IoT device communication
  • Docker: Containerization for scalable deployment

Target Users

This platform serves diverse communities and stakeholders including:

  • FabLabs & Maker Spaces: Monitor equipment energy usage and educate members about sustainability
  • Microfactories: Track manufacturing efficiency and optimize production processes
  • Workshop Facilitators: Use artistic visualizations to engage communities in energy awareness
  • Sustainability Advocates: Generate reports and track environmental impact of making activities
  • Educators & Students: Learn about IoT, energy management, and sustainable manufacturing
  • Artists & Creatives: Explore data visualization and material storytelling
  • Technical Staff: Configure devices, analyze data, and maintain system infrastructure

Demo Environment & Real-World Deployment

This demonstration platform showcases the system validated and deployed across three FabCity Hamburg locations:

  • JUPITER OpenSpace - Community maker space
  • Fabulous St. Pauli - Urban fabrication lab
  • FABRIC Workshop - Production-focused facility

The demo includes:

  • Multiple 3D printer fleets with realistic energy consumption patterns based on actual deployments
  • Historical data covering various operational conditions from site visits and testing
  • Pre-configured dashboards for different user profiles (Staff, Technical, DPP)
  • Sample digital product passports with complete lifecycle information
  • Interactive analysis tools validated through workshop feedback
  • Artistic energy plant visualizations co-created with the Hamburg community

Note: This demo combines real deployment insights with simulated data to demonstrate full system capabilities.

Getting Started

Explore the platform using the navigation menu on the left and the dashboard buttons in the top navigation bar. Each section provides different perspectives on energy management and sustainability metrics:

  • Fleet Operations: Overview of all connected devices and their status
  • Performance Comparison: Side-by-side analysis of equipment efficiency
  • Interactive Analysis: Deep-dive into historical trends and patterns
  • Digital Product Passport (DPP): Product-specific sustainability documentation
  • Node-RED: Configure IoT workflows and data pipelines
