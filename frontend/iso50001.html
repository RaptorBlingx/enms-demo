ISO 50001 Alignment in LAUDS Factories

The ISO 50001:2018 standard provides a shared framework through which organizations of every kind can establish, implement, and continually improve energy management. Its opening statement makes this intent unambiguous: the standard "is applicable to any organization regardless of its type, size, complexity, geographical location, or products and services provided."

Within this understanding, Human-Centric Energy Management System (Human-Centric EnMS) demonstrates how ISO 50001 can be interpreted in FabLabs, maker spaces, and microfactories – the so-called LAUDS Factories – by transforming management clauses into participatory practices. As the standard itself requires documentation of procedures, reviews, forms and records, LAUDS factories are expected to develop that documentation to use along with the Human-Centric EnMS.

The following table summarizes how the Human-Centric EnMS aligns with ISO 50001:2018 requirements within the LAUDS Factories. It highlights which requirements are already implemented through the system, and which remain to be established by the LAUDS Factories by developing the documentation and records.

Legend: ✅ = Implemented through the system | 🌱 = To be established as documentation requirement by LAUDS Factories

Key Achievements in LAUDS Factories

What We Have Accomplished

The Human-Centric EnMS has successfully implemented core technical and participatory elements that align with ISO 50001 requirements:

  • Real-Time Monitoring Infrastructure: ESP32 sensors + Node-RED + TimescaleDB + Grafana for continuous energy measurement
  • Stakeholder Engagement: Multi-profile interfaces (Staff, Technical, DPP) serving makers, educators, lab operators, and community
  • Data-Driven Energy Reviews: Collaborative identification of Significant Energy Uses (SEUs) through workshops and analytics
  • Participatory Improvement Cycles: PDCA implemented via data collection → reviews/workshops → actions → iteration
  • Transparency & Awareness: Artistic feedback, gamification, Digital Product Passports, and open dashboards
  • Measurable Energy Reductions: Reduced idle/standby consumption, better scheduling, improved material efficiency

What Remains to Be Formalized

To achieve full ISO 50001 alignment, LAUDS Factories need to develop formal documentation and procedures:

  • Policy & Governance: Written energy policy signed by management; defined roles (EnMS Coordinator, Data Owner)
  • Risk Management: Lightweight risk/opportunity register for systematic risk assessment
  • Quantified Targets: 2-3 numeric EnPIs (e.g., kWh/print, idle kWh/day) with documented baselines
  • Training Records: Competence matrix and brief training log for staff and volunteers
  • Compliance Checks: Annual legal/regulatory compliance verification and documentation
  • Internal Audits: Convert 2 workshops/year into formal audit cycles with plans and reports
  • Corrective Actions: Simple CA log tracking issues, causes, actions, and verification

Plan-Do-Check-Act (PDCA) Cycle

ISO 50001 is based on the continuous improvement framework known as the PDCA cycle. Our platform supports each phase:

Plan

Define energy baseline, objectives, and action plans using historical analysis and performance metrics.

Do

Implement energy management actions with real-time monitoring and automated data collection.

Check

Analyze results through interactive dashboards, EnPI tracking, and performance comparisons.

Act

Take corrective actions based on insights and continue the improvement cycle with ML predictions.

Energy Performance Indicators (EnPIs)

The platform automatically calculates and tracks multiple EnPIs required by ISO 50001:

Supported EnPIs

  • Total Energy Consumption: Absolute energy use over time (kWh)
  • Specific Energy Consumption (SEC): Energy per unit of production (kWh/unit)
  • Energy Intensity: Energy use normalized by production volume or hours
  • Peak Demand: Maximum power draw during operations (kW)
  • Load Factor: Ratio of average to peak energy demand
  • Energy Cost per Unit: Financial energy performance indicator ($/unit)
  • Carbon Intensity: CO₂ emissions per unit of production (kgCO₂e/unit)

Documentation & Records

ISO 50001 requires maintaining documented information. The platform provides:

  • Energy Reviews: Historical data for conducting mandatory energy reviews
  • Audit Logs: Complete traceability of system changes and user actions
  • Digital Product Passports: Product-level energy consumption documentation
  • Performance Reports: Exportable reports for compliance verification
  • Action Plans: Track improvement initiatives and their outcomes
  • Calibration Records: Sensor and measurement equipment verification

Competence & Awareness (Clause 7.2 & 7.3)

The platform supports organizational competence and awareness requirements through:

  • Role-Based Access: Different user profiles ensure appropriate access levels
  • Interactive Training: Workshop programme module for user education
  • Visual Dashboards: Easy-to-understand visualizations for non-technical staff
  • Alert Systems: Automated notifications for energy anomalies and opportunities
  • Best Practice Sharing: Document successful energy efficiency measures

Journey Towards ISO 50001 Alignment

Our Approach to Energy Management Standards

The Human-Centric EnMS demonstrates a practical implementation pathway for ISO 50001 principles in non-traditional manufacturing environments (FabLabs, maker spaces, microfactories). Our journey shows:

  • Technology Foundation: Robust automated data collection and analytics infrastructure in place
  • Participatory Practices: Active stakeholder engagement replacing traditional top-down mandates
  • Living Documentation: Open repositories and dashboards as real-time evidence of energy performance
  • Continuous Improvement: Demonstrated energy reductions through behavioral changes and system optimization
  • Scalable Model: Proven approach adaptable to diverse organizational contexts and sizes

Next Steps: While core technical and operational elements are implemented, formal documentation and governance structures are being developed to complete full ISO 50001 alignment. This phased approach allows LAUDS Factories to build practical energy management capabilities while progressively formalizing processes.
